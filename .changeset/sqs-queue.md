Implement the World's queue on two SQS standard queues (workflow and step messages, each with a dead-letter queue). `world.start()` runs a worker in the app's process that long-polls both queues, delivers each message to the handler registered for its prefix or over HTTP to the app's route, keeps it invisible while it runs, and deletes it when acknowledged; a failed delivery is retried with a backoff until the queue's `maxReceiveCount` moves it to the dead-letter queue, and `world.close()` waits up to `shutdownGraceMs` for running deliveries. Message ids stay the same across redeliveries, `idempotencyKey` sends are deduplicated for 24 hours on the DynamoDB table, delays longer than 15 minutes are supported, `queueBatch` is available, and `start()` re-enqueues the runs that have not ended. New options, also readable from `WORKFLOW_AWS_*` variables: `pollWaitSeconds` and `baseUrl`, plus variables for `concurrency`, `visibilityTimeoutSeconds`, `shutdownGraceMs` and `streamPollIntervalMs`. Runs that have not ended are indexed in a new sparse GSI3, so listing them no longer reads every run; a table created earlier needs that index. A `hook_disposed` that loses a race with another disposal of the same hook now fails with `EntityConflictError`, as in world-postgres. Streams are not implemented yet.
